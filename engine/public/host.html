Casting

(assign characters to guests in advance)

Type a guest's name against a character. When they join with that name (capitals and extra spaces don't matter), they get that character. A first name on its own is enough unless two guests share it, in which case write both names. Anyone else is seated from what's left. Any flex characters can be used, in any order. Nothing here appears on the gallery screen.

If somebody has already joined and ended up with the wrong character, use the "move to" box on their row. Typing a name against a different character will not move them: once a guest holds a seat, that seat is theirs, which is what stops anybody being re-seated on the night. Their phone keeps working and shows the new character next time it refreshes.

Remove takes a guest out of the party and opens the seat again. Use it when one person has signed up twice under two versions of their name, or when somebody drops out. Their old seat code stops working, and their vote and their 6:40 answer go with them. They can always join again.

The 6:40 question

Ask the room aloud in Phase 3; guests answer on their phones. Then resolve: a contradiction is flagged privately. You won't be told who.

The reveal

Unlocks in Phase 6, once the final vote has closed. It plays on the gallery screen for everyone.

The superlatives

Five awards the room votes on once the reveal has played: best liar, first one you suspected, most 1989, should have been a detective, best costume. Open them with the button and everyone votes on their own phone, for somebody other than themselves.

The winners appear on the room screen, each with the character name and the real first name, so you know whose hand to put a gift card in. A tie shows every tied name rather than picking one. Nobody sees who voted for whom, you included.

When you are ready, announce them. That closes the voting and the room screen walks the five awards one at a time, title first and then the name, with about fifteen seconds on each winner to hand the card over. Best liar goes last. Skip ahead whenever the room has finished reacting.
