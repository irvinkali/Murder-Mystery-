# Reunion 1989: Host Runbook

Everything needed to set up, rehearse and run the night, with **zero spoilers**.
The solution is drawn at random when you start the party and stays sealed inside
the app. No screen shows it to you until the final reveal, so you can play too.

Site: **the-last-exhibit.netlify.app**
The address still says the last exhibit because that is what the project was
called when it held only one game. It is running the reunion. Renaming it would
change the address and break the NFC tags, so leave it alone.

---

## The six phases

| Phase | Name | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrivals | 20 min |
| 2 | The Back Room | 10 min |
| 3 | Asking Around | 35 min |
| 4 | The Hard Part | 25 min |
| 5 | Accusations | 20 min |
| 6 | The Last Slide | 10 min |

Auto-advance is on by default: each phase changes itself when its time runs out,
with a two minute warning first. Turn it off from the host screen if you would
rather drive it by hand, and use Pause and +5 min freely either way.

---

## 1. Two weeks out: casting

Open **the-last-exhibit.netlify.app/host** and press **Create party**. Write
down the two things it gives you:

- the **party code**, four letters, which is what guests type in
- the **host key**, behind the disclosure on the live host card

Keep the host key private and write it down somewhere other than the device you
created the party on. It is the only way back into host controls if that device
dies or you switch machines.

### Reserving characters

In the **Casting** section, type a guest's name against a character. When that
guest joins with that name, capitals and extra spaces ignored, they get that
character. Nothing here ever appears on the room screen.

You do not have to cast everyone. Guests who are not reserved can pick their own
character from a list on the join page, showing only what the whole room will
know about each one. The recommended split, and the reason the casting sheet
rates each role:

- **Reserve the six high pressure roles.** Those need someone who enjoys being
  accused and can keep talking for two hours without getting flustered.
- **Leave the rest open.** Anything you reserve disappears from the guest
  picker, so there is no way for someone to take a seat you have promised.

Cast yourself last, once you see who is left. You can safely take any character,
including one who turns out to have done it, because the app decides that at
random when the game starts and coaches whoever draws it.

### Posting the cast list

The casting sheet gives two lines per character. **The room knows** is safe to
post on the event wall. **Cast someone who** is for you only. Use the name each
guest actually goes by, and use the same spelling in the post and in the app.
Anyone whose name does not match is still seated from what is left, so a typo
costs a mismatched character rather than a locked out guest.

## 2. A week out: props and tags

1. Build the seven props from **`docs/reunion-1989-props-guide.md`**. About $10
   to $12 plus the NFC stickers.
2. Open **/printables**, enter the site address at the top, and print or save as
   PDF. You get the NFC tag map, the item labels and fold inserts, the cue cards
   and a printable invitation.
3. Fold the marked inserts **without reading them**. They are deliberately
   ambiguous; the app tells the real story.
4. Program the seven NFC stickers with the free **NFC Tools** app: Write, add a
   URL record, paste the URL from the tag map. Tap each one to test.
5. Place the props per the guide and tap each one once during setup.

Three of the props are photographs. **Do not use photos of anyone who will be at
the party.** A guest recognizing themselves in a prop breaks the evening.

If a tag misbehaves, guests can always use **I found something** in the app and
type the item number printed on its card. The meaning lives in the app, not on
the sticker.

## 3. A few days out: the dress rehearsal

Grab one other person and two phones. This touches every moving part without
spoiling you. Use a throwaway party, not the real one.

1. **Put the room screen on the television.** Open the Open Room Screen link
   from the host page, or the-last-exhibit.netlify.app/gallery?party=CODE.
   AirPlay it, or run an HDMI cable, which is the more reliable of the two.
   Check that nothing personal appears on it: only the phase, the room and the
   guest list.
2. **Press Enable sound and narration and listen.** There are no pre-recorded
   audio files. Every spoken line is read by the screen device's own voice, so
   this is the first time you will hear her. If she is wrong for the evening,
   change her in the **Narrator voice** dropdown right there; your choice sticks
   on that device.
3. **Join twice**, both phones, with the party code. Each should get a different
   character and a private dossier. Try the character picker on one of them.
4. **Walk the phases** with Advance. At **Asking Around**, one phone privately
   reveals a role change with do and do not guidance. Do not peek at the other
   phone. That is the design working.
5. **Scan a prop**, by tag or by item number. Before **The Hard Part** the
   decisive piece stays held back. Advance to it and scan again; more surfaces.
   That is the fairness guarantee in action.
6. **Run a poll** from the host screen, vote on both phones, close it and see
   the tally. Votes are anonymous.
7. **Try the host key** on a second device. Go to /host on your laptop, open
   "Already started it on another device?", enter the party code and host key,
   and confirm the controls appear and work.
8. **Advance to The Last Slide and play the reveal.** This is the only place and
   time the sealed answer appears. Read it, enjoy it, then press **Forget this
   party on this device** so you walk into the real night unspoiled, and create
   a fresh party for the party.

Checklist: room screen on the TV, narrator audible and right, two joins, phase
advance, the mid game unlock, a scan before and after The Hard Part, a poll
tally, a host takeover, the reveal, then wipe.

## 4. Night of

You set the tempo and read the room; the app is the game master.

| Phase | You do | The app does |
|---|---|---|
| Arrivals | Hand out the party code, help people join, get the room screen up | Seats everyone and hands out private dossiers |
| The Back Room | Gather everyone for the discovery | Opens the case and publishes what the room knows |
| Asking Around | Encourage snooping and prop hunting, run a poll or two, ask the 6:40 question aloud | Privately unlocks the hidden role and serves clues |
| The Hard Part | Keep the energy up, run the pivotal poll | Releases the decisive evidence |
| Accusations | Let people make their case | Opens the final vote and resolves consequences |
| The Last Slide | Play the reveal from the host drawer | Reveals the sealed solution on the room screen |

**The 6:40 question.** Ask the room out loud during Asking Around: *Where were
you at 6:40?* Guests answer on their phones. Then press **Resolve the 6:40
flag**. A contradiction is flagged privately and you are not told to whom.

**Two set pieces**, both in the host drawer, both once per night, both yours to
time: **Blackout** drops every screen in the room to dark, and **Portrait** calls
everyone together for a group photo. Use them when the energy needs a jolt.

**Polls are your main lever.** Good ones: who benefits most, who are you most
suspicious of, whose story does not add up. Read the options off the roster.

**Quiet guests?** Walk over and hand them a small task out of their own dossier.
Every dossier has hooks for this.

## 5. When something goes wrong

- **A tag will not scan.** Use I found something and the item number. Keep one
  spare sticker programmed as a backup.
- **A guest's phone dies.** Their dossier page shows a five letter **seat code**.
  On any phone, open the site, expand Rejoining, and enter the party code and
  seat code to get the same character back.
- **Your host device dies.** Open /host on any other device, expand "Already
  started it on another device?", and enter the party code and host key.
- **More or fewer guests than planned.** Anything from 10 to 20 works, and the
  mystery is provably solvable at every size. That is checked automatically on
  every deploy.
- **The narrator sounds wrong.** Change her in the dropdown on the room screen.
- **You need to start over mid setup.** Forget this party on this device, then
  create a fresh one.

---

## 6. The promise

The killer is drawn **at random when you start the party**. You do not know it.
Claude does not know it. It cannot leak, because it is not decided until the
night and never appears on any screen before the last phase.

Every prop matters in at least one version of the truth, so nothing is safely
ignored. Every guest is plausibly guilty. The decisive clue cannot arrive early.

You get to actually play. Have a wonderful, petty, thirty-five years overdue
evening.
