# The Last Exhibit — Props Guide (Spoiler-Safe)

Seven props. Each will either carry genuinely important information or convincingly appear to. Which is which depends on the solution variant selected at game start — so nothing on this page tells you anything about the plot. Do not add, remove, or substitute props without telling me, since each is woven into the evidence system.

Each prop gets a clear NFC sticker (write the URL I provide to it using the free "NFC Tools" app). Props marked [placard] instead get a small "Exhibit No. __" card, styled like gallery signage — I'll provide a printable sheet.

## Placement Guide

Semi-hidden means visible to someone poking around, invisible to someone standing in the middle of the room. Guests finding these "too early" is fine — the app handles early discoveries gracefully.

- **P1 (flute):** On a shelf, windowsill, or side table away from the actual drinks station — somewhere a glass looks abandoned, not staged.
- **P2 (planner):** Tucked mostly under a couch cushion or into a bookshelf with a corner showing.
- **P3 (pill bottle):** Bathroom — medicine cabinet or windowsill. People always snoop in the bathroom.
- **P4 (sealed envelope):** Slipped behind or taped to the back of one of your framed "artworks," with an edge visible.
- **P5 (gloves):** Draped over or near wherever you stage the "final installation" area, or by a utility/closet door.
- **P6 (photograph):** Inside a book on a coffee table, or in the coat/bag drop area.
- **P7 (key fob):** Under the edge of a rug, or on the floor half-under furniture near an exterior door — like it was dropped.

## Night-of Notes

- Place props during setup, after decor is done. Takes 5 minutes.
- If a prop gets picked up and moved by a guest before the game starts, don't worry — the app ties meaning to the item, not the location.
- Keep one spare NFC sticker on hand in case one fails a test tap.
- Test-tap each placed prop once with your phone during setup (the app will show a neutral "Exhibit catalog" page pre-game — no spoilers).
